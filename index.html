📋 PDF Information

✨ Features

This PDF Page Shuffle tool is a web application that allows you to randomly reorder the pages of a PDF file. Since it runs entirely in your browser, your files are not sent to a server, ensuring complete privacy. You can protect pages you don't want to move, such as the cover or back page, with the fix option. A new feature to shuffle while preserving spreads (2-page units) has been added, making it suitable for book-like PDFs. After processing, the file is available for immediate download, with '_shuffled' appended to the original file name.

How to shuffle PDF pages

  1. Drag and drop your PDF, or click "Choose file".
  2. If needed, choose options such as keeping the cover in place or shuffling by spreads.
  3. Click "Shuffle Pages".
  4. Click "Download Shuffled PDF" to save the shuffled PDF.

Great for studying and more

  • Exam preparation: randomize flashcards, question banks, and past exams in PDF so you stop memorizing answers by page order.
  • Language learning: reorder phrase lists and example sentence cards to keep repeated practice fresh.
  • Training and presentations: randomize the order of quiz materials and case studies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free. All features are available without registration or limits.

Are my files secure? Is my privacy protected?

Yes, it's secure. This tool operates entirely within your browser. Your PDF files are never uploaded to a server, ensuring your privacy is fully protected.

Can I use any PDF file?

Most standard PDF files are supported. However, password-protected or specially formatted files may not be processed correctly.

Can I undo the shuffle?

The shuffling process does not alter your original file. If you are not satisfied with the result, simply reload the page, upload the original file again, and try shuffling once more.

Can I shuffle by 2-page units (spreads)?

Yes, there are several spread-aware options, only one of which can be selected at a time.
• 'Fix page 1 (shuffle by spreads)': Fixes the cover (p. 1) and shuffles the body in pairs like pages 2-3, 4-5, etc.
• 'Fix pages 1-2 (shuffle by spreads)': Fixes the first spread (pp. 1-2) and shuffles the body in pairs like pages 3-4, 5-6, etc.
• 'Shuffle all by spreads': Shuffles the entire PDF in pairs like pages 1-2, 3-4, etc.
If no spread option is chosen, pages are shuffled individually.
